# Dual-morph animation and export

Apply a supported dual morph, open Animation, set **Morph ratio endpoint**, and choose **Add dual-morph ratio sweep**. The start ratio is zero; the end is the entered value. Move the time slider and adjust the Morph pose slider to save additional ratio keyframes. Existing rotation keyframes remain available. Capture **Base model** as PNG frames or WebM. Animated tour snapshots retain and evaluate this track.

Each sample runs the native prepared-source evaluator at the interpolated ratio. The Viewer adopts the resulting geometry and mapped source labels/PNG sheets atomically after pixel preparation. This does not change document vertices, ordered incidence, notes, source attributes, or content ownership. Export restores the starting morph ratio, view angles, camera controls and saved view on success, cancellation and output failure unless an external edit invalidated ownership. An external source/camera edit cancels publication and is retained.

The sequence contract is version **3**, with `tracks.morphRatio = {kind: 'dual-morph', version: 1}` and a finite `morphRatio` in `[0,1]` on every keyframe. Versions 1 and 2 retain their previous interpretation. Track method, center and radius remain in the saved source `view.dualMorph`. The current native method/dimension eligibility is reused: all eight supported methods in intrinsic 3D; expansion and tilting quads in intrinsic convex 4D; the already qualified generalized domains retain their existing eligibility. Via-snub remains unavailable.

Source text uses declared moving ownership including reciprocal endpoints. Face PNG requires a complete affine source-corner sheet: a track ending at a rank change without such a sheet is refused before an output session begins. Intermediate samples repeat qualification and may refuse a newly unavailable mapping without replacing the previous complete pose. Choose a supported interior interval for PNG-covered faces. Native codec/video output remains approximate and uncertified.

Folding or explosion combined with a morph ratio track, explosive morph tour transitions, changing section depth, morph section capture, and split source-section morph tours are explicitly unavailable. Other tour presentation transitions use the evaluated morph geometry. The caller's explicit camera is preserved; large swept poses can extend beyond its frame. Adjust the camera before capture. Automatic swept fitting is not provided by this stage.

The primary [Stella Morphing manual](https://mail.software3d.com/Manual/Morphing.php?prod=Stella4DPro), [Animation manual](https://www.software3d.com/Manual/Animate.php), and [Export Image manual](https://mail.software3d.com/Manual/ExportImage.php?prod=Stella4DPro) ground ratio animation, combined rotation and image/video export. This implementation does not claim universal method or combination parity.
