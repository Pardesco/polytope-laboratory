# Generalized source face nets

The existing net editor already provides source-edge cuts/joins, rigid connected-piece dragging/rotation, saved Undo/Redo, folding, tabs and physical SVG/PDF page packing. This extension removes its convex-volume prerequisite for eligible sources without replacing the supplied geometry.

The source must be an intrinsic affine 3D, closed orientable shell with exactly two distinct faces per edge and one circular face link per vertex. Disconnected closed shell components are permitted. Convex and concave **simple planar** source faces are supported. Source vertex/edge/face IDs and every supplied ordered cycle remain unchanged. Intrinsic 3D shells embedded in 4D use a saved orthonormal source chart. Toroidal topology is allowed; Euler characteristic two is not assumed.

Face normals come from supplied cycles and coherent edge-incidence orientation, rather than a vector away from the source centroid. This permits reflex dihedrals and toroidal surfaces. Folding uses rigid source-edge hinge rotations and verifies source reconstruction, lengths and joined hinges. Concave overlap checks use positive-area ear triangles; the missing region of an L-shaped face is not counted as filled. Glue tabs use the cycle's signed orientation at each edge and include overlap with their owning concave face in the diagnostics. Native source-corner triangles are also used by the folding Viewer, including when the fill setting changes, so concave paint points cannot remain in the flat layout.

The established convex kernel remains the route for `convex-polytope` sources. The generalized route preserves E0 millimeters, annotations/embedded PNG, original source state and notes, matching edge IDs, explicit placement, and native saved net history. `net`, `net-edit`, `net-fold` and `net-pack` all reconstruct through the same source-aware router. Reopening a matching saved history regenerates the layout through the existing NetEditor. No new application mode or document format is required.

The extension refuses self-crossing/touching/retracing face cycles, nonplanar faces, nonorientable or pinched incidence, open edges, unused entities and cyclic hinge graphs. It never repairs these by taking a hull. Limits are 250 faces, 128 vertices per face and 4,000 face-boundary vertices. Float64 predicates are approximate. Positive face/tab overlaps are reported, not silently removed. Distinct source-face intersections, free-space folding collision avoidance, optimal paper packing, star-face sheet construction and generalized 4D cell nets are not certified. Existing support reinforcement/dihedral measurement operations retain their separate convex domain.

The practical benchmark is Stella's [Build manual, sections 7.1–7.1.1](https://www.software3d.com/Manual/Build.php), which describes nets/preview, matching connection IDs, physical printing and folding. Its [color separation options](https://www.software3d.com/Manual/Build.php) also explicitly distinguish nonconvex models. These references motivate useful source-shell coverage; they do not establish unrestricted Stella parity or guarantee an overlap-free net for every model.

Targeted checks use independent literal coordinates/incidence: an L-prism with concave caps and inconsistent supplied face orientation; a planar-quad torus with Euler characteristic zero; and an affine 3D-in-4D embedding. They exercise true face area, missing-face interior overlap, reentrant tab direction, source-corner triangle area, literal text/PNG, cuts/joins/placement, rigid folding endpoints/intermediate poses, all-face paper packing, native history persistence, source refusal and unchanged convex routing. Two small Node checks validate folding corner ownership and disconnected face offsets. No GUI or full conformance suite is claimed.
