Guide
Construction and fitting. Construct includes expansion/runcination for supported convex 3D and 4D sources. Fit geometric conditions previews regular-face, equal-edge, or equal-area solutions with residuals. Adopt a valid result; accepting unresolved targets creates a documented near miss.
Labels and metadata. Open Inspector → Analysis for automatic source IDs, edge lengths, coordinates, and incidence labels. Preview then Apply; existing text and face images are preserved by default. Project metadata edits the document title, author, references, and extra fields. JSON geometry export retains those fields and notes; native projects also retain presentation and history.
Viewing. Multiple views opens one to six independent cameras with linked picking and a whole-grid PNG export. Appearance → Material and lighting provides material presets, tint, highlights, light positions, and viewport themes. The stereo selector offers red/cyan, parallel, and cross-eyed viewing.
Library search. Combine words or quoted phrases with count filters, for example dim:4 v>=24 c:8. Missing catalog properties do not match numeric filters.
Equations. Use cosd(60), 1+3r2 or diag(5/2). phi, tau and g denote the golden ratio. Polygon helpers interpret n/d as a polygon symbol. Products and division have equal precedence; use parentheses to group a denominator.
Memories and orientation. Store/open/swap models or derived results in nine project memories. Add creates a compound using literal source coordinates. First/Last orients a selected 4D entity toward or away from the W observer. Open also accepts supported ASCII DXF faces, meshes, lines and points.
Choose a model from the library, including snub mirror forms, Catalan duals and the four regular star polyhedra. The base view and the derived view share the same source geometry. Star models preserve self-crossing face order. Choose nonzero or even/odd face fill, opacity, source colors, and cell visibility without changing the source geometry. Drag either view to orbit; scroll to zoom. Six independent sliders rotate 4D display coordinates.
View controls. Rotate starts continuous coordinate rotation in both 3D and 4D; choose its plane and angular speed in Rotation. Drag to orbit the 3D camera and scroll to zoom. In 4D, Shift-drag rotates XW and YW display coordinates; enable Drag in 4D to use ordinary dragging for this. Camera: parallel uses a true orthographic 3D camera, while Camera: perspective uses perspective foreshortening. The separate 4D projection selector maps four-dimensional display coordinates into three dimensions. Fit frames the model, and Isometric or axis views set the camera direction. Model + derived opens a comparison viewport. Library and Inspector can be hidden to give geometry the full workspace.
Construct exposes polar duals, convex edge-cut truncation, extrusion, scaling, reflection, product families, and point-cloud hulls. The separate 3D incidence dual reciprocates face planes and follows vertex links, preserving star winding and genus without convexifying the boundary. Each committed result becomes an undoable history state. New generators open a separate document.
Sections use n · x = depth, with a normalized normal in intrinsic coordinates. They remain independent of display rotation. Generalized sections preserve source-face intersection curves and coplanar faces; they do not assign a filled solid interior. Tangent points and empty sections have explicit statuses. Choose a cell or vertex figure by index, then promote a derived result into its own document.
Stellation computes facial-plane regions for convex 3D seeds. Select enclosed regions, inspect a facial-plane diagram, or enumerate unions under explicit symmetry, seed-inclusion and facet-connectivity criteria. Increase observation scale when bounded regions are clipped. Promote the selected solid to section it without convexifying it.
Projects save full geometry, history, camera/display state, and notes. Ctrl+S opens the native save dialog. Autosave runs every 30 seconds; File → Recover autosave restores the last snapshot. OFF/4OFF files retain face and cell incidence; linked catalogs are read lazily without changing source files. Attach an audit report for passed, rejected, untested and stale badges; Verify checks current file hashes. Validated edge-count repairs are saved as separate copies. OFF RGB/RGBA source colors are retained.
Export writes intrinsic geometry. 4D models support OFF and JSON; promote a 3D section/cell for OBJ, STL, DXF edges, VRML or POV-Ray. Image export captures the base view. Animation tracks save rotation and section-depth keyframes with the project. PNG sequences use exact frame times; WebM records in real time, so slow section evaluations can lengthen playback. The Nets inspector edits cuts, joins and connected-piece placements, and previews rigid folding. SVG/PDF net exports use physical millimeters. Standard-page packing keeps connected pieces intact and previews every page at the chosen scale. Whole-cell 4D nets retain every complete 3D cell, with face connections, placements, matching source elements and display-only surface shrink. Face and tab overlaps are reported separately; cell intersections are currently ignored.
Numeric guarantees: geometric operations are approximate float64. The evidence tab lists structural checks. 2D/3D/4D symmetry exhausts compatible vertex frames and checks full source incidence and generator closure numerically. Proper rotations, setwise entity stabilizers, entity orbits and action matrices are available. Signed-axis comparison remains a separate subgroup method. Full Stella4D parity, nonconvex constructions, exhaustive stellation/faceting, generalized nets, and hardware printer calibration remain under development.
Expressions: + − * / ^, pi, e, phi, sqrt, sin/cos/tan (radians), deg, asin/acos/atan, abs, min/max, exp/log, floor/ceil, chord(n). No arbitrary code is evaluated.
