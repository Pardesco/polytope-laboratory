# 0.25 development

The [0.25 community preview](RELEASE_0.25_PREVIEW.md) and corresponding GPLv3
source archive are frozen and locally ready for GitHub publication. These
workflows extend the prior 0.24 checkpoint.

- Expansion morphs of finite closed ordinary star/nonconvex intrinsic 3D
  boundaries. Original ordered faces, reciprocal vertex-link caps and actual
  four-flag edge bridges are retained. All four regular stars and a concave
  L-prism pass planarity, polarity, edge closure, endpoint and ownership checks.
  Source text and eligible affine PNG sheets follow their declared owners.
- Automatic full or proper geometric source groups for recorded faceting
  searches. Exact metric-compatible bijections are exhaustively checked against
  full source incidence. Rational affine matrices, permutations, discovery work
  and complete/interrupted evidence are retained and reconstructed on replay.
  An interrupted discovery cannot supply a quotient group. This concerns literal
  supplied coordinates: rounded nominal regular sources can have fewer exact
  symmetries. Numerical/symbolic interoperation remains separate work.
- [Physical glue-tab controls](NET_TAB_CONTROLS.md): default/per-source-edge
  no, single or double tabs; explicit single-tab source-face choice; reset to the
  default; and width in millimeters. Editing, cached layouts, native history,
  annotations, packing and tours retain the source-bound preferences. Cutting
  and joining keep dormant overrides. Coincident-edge assembly and separate
  folded-tab rendering remain unsupported.
- [Literal 4D incidence reciprocation](INCIDENCE_DUAL_4D.md), including finite
  generalized and star source boundaries. Actual cyclic source-edge links form
  the dual faces and complete source-vertex neighborhoods form the dual cells.
  Four-coordinate expression entry, rank/color maps, source archives, native
  Save/Open and operation history retain their guards.
- [Exact regular source choices](EXACT_REGULAR_SOURCES.md) for tetrahedron,
  cube and octahedron faceting. Integer coordinates, independently supplied
  outward cycles and rational support tables carry actual full/proper source
  groups 24/12, 48/24 and 48/24. These are alternate realizations of existing
  solids; source choice, adoption, Save/Open and history replay retain their
  geometry and attributes.
- [Source-color paper printing](NET_COLOR_PRINTING.md): automatic, mixed or
  one-color-per-net/page packing, selected paper-color batches and optional
  face color ink. Separate mode cuts cross-color hinges in a detached print
  forest, retaining original net edits, matching IDs, physical tabs, text and
  PNG images. Four-face coincident-edge assembly remains open.
- [Generalized 4D expansion](GENERALIZED_4D_EXPANSION.md) uses actual source
  vertex/cell flags and complete source/polar product cells, including finite
  concave and regular-star sources. Original winding, rank colors, source text
  and eligible affine PNG face sheets retain their declared ownership. Large
  annotated packets and singular or unsupported image poses diagnose limits.
- [Generalized 3D fold animation](GENERALIZED_FOLD_ANIMATION.md) supports
  concave/toroidal face nets in saved keyframes, animated tours and PNG/WebM
  export. Native reconstruction retains edited forests, source text/PNG,
  physical tabs and complete simple-planar face triangulation.

The mounted build passes. Eight native groups cover expansion, tab geometry,
4D duality, source preservation and native persistence. Six Viewer/session/controller
groups pass. Automatic source-symmetry native/UI sanity checks pass, including
independent cube48/tetra24/asymmetric1 evidence, translated affine matrices,
incidence-restricted sources, interrupted discovery and native adoption/replay.
These checks do not claim a full regression or measured 90% parity.

The short actual desktop check passes four-coordinate 4D reciprocation and PNG
capture, exact source catalog choices, double 5mm tabs and single-side override,
selected source-RGBA paper batches, rotations-only tetrahedral tidy-dual search
and adoption, and generalized star expansion capture/Save/Open. It records no
renderer errors in `artifacts/feature-0.25-quick-NuAPGG/result.json`. Three native
color-printing groups and two frontend groups also pass. Exact-source native
catalog/adoption/replay and catalog-query checks pass.

Generalized 4D expansion passes three native and two Viewer/session groups.
The actual desktop check passes concave 4D expansion, moving text on all four
source ranks, two moving PNG poses, native Save/Open and retained prior pose
after unsupported endpoint refusal:
`artifacts/generalized-4d-morph-quick-zxan0U/result.json`.

Generalized folding passes two independent native source checks and six
Viewer/animation/tour checks. The actual desktop check exports three PNG poses
at 0, 0.5 and 1 seconds, verifies three decoded WebM frames, and preserves source
geometry, RGBA, units, annotations, net history and physical tab preferences
through Save/Open: `artifacts/generalized-fold-quick-FEbVM9/result.json`.

Next isolated work expands complete generalized 4D cell sections, four-face
coincident-edge assembly, and dual-morph animation/video export. The
original broad feature-complete goal remains active.
