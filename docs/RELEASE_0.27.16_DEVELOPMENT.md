# 0.27.16: current measurement and regular-identity feedback

Local development preview, October 22, 2026. Feature expansion remains paused.
Public production remains 0.26.0; nothing was published online. Prior local
portable/source pairs remain preserved.

Simple vertex distance and three-vertex angle measurements now own their source
and the literal kind/index fields. Editing either field clears the previous
result and cancels a pending check; a late reply cannot describe different input.
Fresh checks show progress, explicit Cancel stays quiet and retries use the
current indices. Genuine current native refusal messages remain available.

Verify regular 4D identity clears its previous receipt while checking. Notes,
coordinate-unit display declarations and observer motion do not change the
intrinsic check. Switching models cancels pending feedback without placing a
stale error on the new source. Complete literal model content and selected
project/document/state still bind publication; IDs/fingerprints alone do not.
No native algorithms, coordinates, domains or numerical claims were changed.

The two small query channels cancel independently. Global Cancel affects their
pending work while retaining unrelated completed reports. Existing analysis and
incidence validation retain their channel ordering. Advanced measurement and
faceting ownership guards remain enforced. The offline guide explains the flow.
