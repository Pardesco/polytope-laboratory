# Generalized 4D expansion

Dual morph **Expansion** now accepts finite closed ordinary intrinsic 4D
boundaries with a resolved incidence reciprocal, including supported nonconvex
and regular-star sources. Other generalized 4D morph methods remain unavailable.

For each actual source vertex/cell flag, its moving vertex is
`C + (1-t)(Pv-C) + t(Qc-C)`, where `Qc` is the reciprocal of that source cell's
plane. Output faces and complete cells use products of actual source and polar
incidence. Original ordered cycles and star winding remain literal. No hull,
coordinate weld or filled-volume interpretation replaces the source boundary.

Every output face and complete cell must have the expected planar rank, closure
and local links. Mixed faces are orthogonal rectangles. Exact endpoints return
the complete original or reciprocal model; unresolved near-collapsed interior
ratios are refused. Source attributes, notes, units and rank-owned colors remain
recorded. This is approximate numerical geometry with explicit residuals.

Source text follows its declared rank owners. PNG faces map only to complete
affine original-face sheets, retaining ordered corners. Images do not attach to
mixed rectangles or arbitrary reciprocal faces. Unsupported mappings or collapsed
projected image frames refuse publication while retaining the previous display.
Rotate the 4D view when an otherwise valid image face is projected edge-on.

Native construction limits are 20,000 flags, 32,000 elements per rank and one
million output incidence visits, in addition to incidence-dual input limits.
Large regular-star packets can exceed the browser's unchanged content-signature
work limit when annotations are requested; those annotated poses explicitly
refuse. Full color interpolation, infinite/hemi reciprocals and generalized
tilting methods remain separate work.

Focused native checks compare 5-cell and tesseract output to independent convex
expansion, and cover a supplied concave product plus an attributed grand 600-cell
and its reciprocal. Both star directions produce 2,400 vertices, 7,200 edges,
7,440 faces and 2,640 complete cells, preserving pentagram winding two. Native
Save/Open and two Viewer/session checks pass.

The real desktop check in
`artifacts/generalized-4d-morph-quick-zxan0U/result.json` passes concave 4D
expansion, all four source text ranks, two moving poses, affine face PNG capture,
Save/Open and atomic refusal at an unsupported PNG endpoint.
