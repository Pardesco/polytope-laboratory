# Ordinary nonconvex cells in 4D nets

The whole-cell source domain now includes literal nonconvex 3D cells whose boundaries are connected, closed, orientable shells of simple planar source faces. Face cycles may themselves be concave. All source cell IDs, ordered face ownership, vertex IDs, complete face cycles and metric distances remain intact. A cell is never replaced by its convex hull or by multiple tetrahedral cells.

This follows the [primary Stella manual, section 15.8](https://www.software3d.com/Manual/Net4D.php): 4D nets contain complete intact cells joined at faces, and intersections between arranged cells are ignored. Its selection, reattachment, shrink, remembered rearrangement and separate-cell gestures already exist here; this extension broadens the source cell domain using those same workflows.

Each cell is projected into its actual intrinsic affine 3D chart solely for validation. Shared-edge incidence propagates a coherent orientation of its supplied face cycles. A planar-face divergence integral over those complete cycles chooses the boundary's global orientation. This scalar is an approximate orientation check, not a solid-volume certificate or replacement-cell construction. It avoids the assumption that a nonconvex cell's vertex average lies on the inward side of every face. The oriented face-normal complement also supplies coplanar hinge half-turn axes; no centroid heuristic is needed.

Local boundaries must have two distinct faces at every edge, one circular face link at every vertex, connected face incidence, nonzero resolved orientation volume and intrinsic affine rank three. Global source coordinates must span four dimensions; every source face must join two distinct cells; global edge links remain circular and vertex links spherical/connected. The original bounded orientation/manifold/source checks remain in place. Connected genus boundaries can be qualified; global cell solidity or source embeddedness is not inferred.

All independently full-convex-cell sources retain the previously reviewed generalized whole-cell route and its output, including algorithm version and exact layout. Other qualified ordinary shells use the new route. The established globally convex source route is unchanged. Both share existing native reconstruction/edit functions and source-ID/fingerprint checks. Native Save/Open rebuilds cached layout geometry from source-bound root/connections/placements through frozen importer code. Existing saved history, annotation documents, text/embedded PNG, source RGBA, notes and units remain source owned.

Native face-corner ear triangles are display data only. The whole-cell Viewer retains each original cell and its full faces; display triangles reference duplicated owner-cell source corners. Thus a concave face's fill follows the same shrink positions as the cell's ordinary face corners, while mathematical cells, source edges and face cycles retain their size/incidence. The existing capture and tour owner mappings continue to receive unchanged complete-cell records.

Explicit refusals include crossing/touching/retraced or nonplanar face cycles, open/pinched/nonorientable shells, disconnected multiply shelled individual cells, unresolved rank and zero/unresolved orientation volume. Nested cavities represented by disconnected boundary shells are unsupported. Self-intersections between distinct faces of a source cell, source-cell intersections and intersections between arranged cells are **not certified or removed**. No overlap-free net, solid-union volume, collision-free folding, unrestricted star-cell coverage or printer construction of a 4D solid is claimed.

Approximate limits: 1,024 cells, 250 faces/256 vertices per cell, 128 vertices per face, 4,000 local cell face-boundary vertices, 100,000 global face-boundary vertices and 20,000 cell-vertex incidences. Existing float64 scale/rank tolerances apply.

Focused fixtures: a prism on a literal inward-dented tetrahedron (two intact nonconvex cap cells, each orientation volume 1/10 rather than a convex replacement's 1/6), and a prism on a literal L-prism (four nonconvex cells including concave faced cells). They check full ordered incidence, every cell distance, actual joined face vertices, cut/move/join/reattach, text/PNG/RGBA/history/notes, regenerated native cached layouts, explicit bad-domain/zero-volume/source refusal and exact compatibility with full-convex-cell outputs. Small display checks retain both owner copies and true L-face area while using cell-corner shrink positions. Actual protocol plus native Save/Open is checked in the isolated stage. No GUI or exhaustive matrix is claimed.
