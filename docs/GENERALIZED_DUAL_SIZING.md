# Sizing morphs of closed star and nonconvex 3D boundaries

Sizing shrinks the original layer while growing its reciprocal. The method now
accepts finite closed ordinary intrinsic 3D generalized boundaries, including
the four Kepler-Poinsot regular stars and nonconvex simple-faced shells.

The finite reciprocal comes from actual source face planes and ordered source
vertex links. Source-edge indices are restored from the explicit correspondence.
Original face cycles and winding, reciprocal link cycles, colors and alpha remain
literal. Interior scales remain 1-ratio and ratio; the endpoints are the literal
original and reciprocal models. Stable receipts retain source ownership.

Planar faces, two distinct faces per edge and one circular face link per vertex
are required. Planes through the reciprocal center, coincident reciprocal owners,
nonplanar/open/nonmanifold/flat or numerically unresolved inputs are refused.
No supporting-hull enclosure or filled-solid interpretation is substituted.
Genus and self-intersection are not silently repaired.

Source work limits are 4,000 vertices, 2,048 face planes and 32,000 edges; the
existing combined-sizing limits also apply. Only sizing receives this expanded
domain. Other generalized methods and additional 4D methods remain separate work.

Three native groups check independent regular-star reciprocal vertex orbits,
ordered links, exact endpoints, rank/color transfer, deterministic receipts,
dual-dual coordinates, a genuinely concave prism and bad-domain refusals. Two
Viewer/session groups check moving content and PNG sheets on all four stars,
seeking, capture and source guards. Native Save/Open is included.
