# Tidy faceting and its finite polar dual

In Automatic facets, choose **Tidy faceting and finite polar dual**. The search
requires both the selected primal boundary and its actual finite reciprocal to
satisfy the strict source-incidence tidy predicate. A plane through the center
can make the reciprocal infinite even when the primal is tidy.

An empty center resolves to the mean of the complete source vertex set, including
unused vertices in a partial result. An explicit center accepts XYZ expressions.
The positive reciprocal radius is bounded from 0.0001 to 10,000. Center coordinates
are bounded by 1e100. For subgroup equivalence, the center must be fixed by the
declared source isometries.

The exact predicate builds polar points and dual faces from complete ordered
source vertex links. Independent numerical native incidence-dual construction
must retain those cycles and agree with the exact polar coordinates within 1e-7
of dual span. Native geometry and face/vertex polarity are also checked. Unused
vertices are compressed only inside a temporary carrier with explicit maps;
the full source and adopted result retain their original owners.

Exactly infinite face planes yield a complete negative result. Coincident polar
owners, finite near-singular/native unsupported geometry, unresolved links and
insufficient reciprocal work remain unknown. Such cases interrupt an exhaustive
search claim; they are never silently added to its rejected count. No vertex weld,
replacement hull or angular face sort is used.

The optional controls use the existing search receipts, adoption, operation
history and replay. Searches without this criterion retain their previous
semantics. The original recorded candidate, node and result limits still apply;
broader faceting enumeration remains separate work.
